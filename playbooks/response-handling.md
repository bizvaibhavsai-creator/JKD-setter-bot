# Response Handling Playbook

Replace this file with your full response playbook, or switch to `PLAYBOOK_SOURCE=google_doc`.

## Core goals

- classify inbound replies correctly
- mirror the prospect's tone
- keep replies concise and action-oriented
- avoid robotic wording
- do not auto-send low-confidence replies

## Default classification set

- interested
- not_interested
- info_request
- out_of_office
- referral
- maybe_later
- objection
- wrong_person
- meeting_request
- complaint_angry
- bounced_invalid
- competitive
- conditional
- positive_no
- follow_up_needed

## Key rules

- acknowledge specifics from the reply before pitching
- honor opt-outs immediately
- do not argue with not interested replies
- for interested replies, drive toward a booking
- for information requests, answer clearly and keep the next step simple
